How it works
1

Encode

A bidirectional LSTM reads the English sentence both ways and gives every word a context-aware vector hⱼ.

2

Score

Before each French word, the decoder scores how relevant every English word is.

eᵢⱼ = vᵀ tanh(W sᵢ₋₁ + U hⱼ)
3

Attend

A softmax turns scores into weights that sum to 1, and mixes the encoder states into a context.

cᵢ = Σⱼ αᵢⱼ hⱼ
4

Decode

An LSTM uses that context to pick the next French word; beam search keeps the best few sentences.
